Don't Miss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Don't Miss is a free trick-shot game. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no server of its own. This policy explains the only ways data leaves your device: your own iCloud, Apple's Game Center, advertising, and the optional purchase.

What stays on your device

Your Campaign progress (stars, crowns, skipped stages), your Arcade and Daily Run bests, and your settings (music, sounds, vibrations) are stored on your iPhone, using the operating system's standard app storage. We never receive them, and deleting the app deletes the copy on your device.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress and bests are also kept in your own iCloud account, so they follow you to your other devices and survive a reinstall. This data is stored by Apple, in your account, under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it. "Reset Progress" in the game's settings erases it everywhere.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, your Arcade and Daily Run scores are submitted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center identity. This is entirely optional — the game is identical if you decline — and the data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

In-app purchase

The game offers one optional in-app purchase, "Full Game", which opens every world after the first and removes full-screen ads. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your name, your payment details or your purchase history; the app only asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns the purchase, so it can be restored on a new device.

Advertising

The game shows two kinds of ads, served by Google AdMob:

  • Occasional full-screen ads after an Arcade or Daily Run game over — never during the Campaign, and never if you own the "Full Game".
  • Optional rewarded videos, only when you ask for one: to continue an Arcade run, to see a hint, or to skip a stage. These stay available to every player, including "Full Game" owners, because they are always your choice.

To serve and measure these ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with the ads.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy & Terms and in how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are served, through Google's consent form. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the game.
  • On iOS, the game also asks for permission to use the advertising identifier (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). Declining changes nothing about the game: you will still see ads, they will simply be less relevant. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Don't Miss is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes
